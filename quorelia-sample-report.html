02 · Scope & methodology

Data ingestion & validation pipeline

Every figure in this report is produced by the same automated pipeline, on top of the SCADA and historian systems already deployed at the facility — no rip-and-replace, no additional hardware.

1

Plant data ingestion

Continuous polling of SCADA, PPC, and BMS/BESS historian channels — inverters, strings, trackers, meters, and battery racks — at native resolution.

2

Data validation

Every channel is checked against expected behaviour: range limits, rate-of-change bounds, cross-channel consistency, and gap detection. Each data point is tagged Nominal, Warning, Fault, or Not observed before any KPI is calculated.

3

KPI calculation & data quality engine

Availability, performance ratio, and energy figures are computed only from data that passes validation. Where coverage falls below the defensible threshold for a given period, the figure is withheld rather than estimated.

4

Verification engine

Each published figure carries its source channel, its transformation, and its data-coverage percentage — reproducible back to the raw measurement, on request.

5

Report & delivery

Daily and monthly reports are generated automatically, in client-branded PDF and portal formats, in English, Spanish, or any language sourced for the engagement.

05 · Findings & exceptions

Findings & exceptions

Exceptions identified during validation for the reporting period, in order of materiality.

F-01 — Data coverage below threshold, INV-11Withheld

Communication loss between the block-11 datalogger and the plant historian from 14–28 Jan (14.2 days), reducing period coverage to 48.8% — below the 90% threshold for a defensible availability figure. Availability for INV-11 is withheld for this period rather than estimated.

Recommendation: replace or reconfigure the block-11 datalogger's communication module; Quorelia will re-certify the period once coverage is restored and back-filled data is validated.
F-02 — Elevated warning rate, INV-06 (6–9 Jan)Warning

String-level current imbalance flagged on 6 occasions over a 72-hour window, consistent with a soiling pattern rather than a fault. Did not affect certified availability.

Recommendation: schedule a cleaning cycle for row 6 ahead of the next reporting period.
F-03 — Short data gap, BESS-01 (22 Jan, 04:10–05:55)Warning

1h 45m telemetry gap during a scheduled SCADA maintenance window, pre-notified by the client. Excluded from coverage denominator per methodology Section 02.

No action required — handled per standard scheduled-maintenance exclusion.

06 · Independent reviewer's note

Independent reviewer's note

A market and technical context review, performed independently of the automated verification pipeline in Sections 02–05.

"A 96.2% certified availability, on a 1 GWac-class hybrid asset, sits comfortably ahead of the regional benchmark range for first-three-years operation in northern Chile, where curtailment exposure and dust-driven soiling losses typically weigh on reported PR more than equipment failure does. The single withheld block in this period is a communications issue, not a generation issue — the distinction matters, because it is the difference between a defensible number and a number that simply wasn't checked closely enough to know.

The discipline worth noting here isn't the 96.2% — it's the 48.8%. Most commercial monitoring stacks would either quietly interpolate that gap or report a number that implies more confidence than the data supports. Withholding it, and naming the root cause on the first page rather than in an appendix, is the behaviour an asset owner, lender, or offtake counterparty actually needs to see before they can rely on a platform's output without re-checking it themselves."

07 · Appendix

Definitions & methodology

State legend

Nominal

Measured, verified, and within expected range.

Warning

Threshold breach or anomaly flagged; does not affect certified availability unless it persists.

Fault

Confirmed equipment or channel fault, traced to source and timestamped.

Critical

Fault with material impact on availability or safety; routed for immediate escalation.

Not observed

The channel never responded for the period. Drawn as a hatch pattern — never reported as zero.

Withheld

Data exists but falls below the coverage threshold required to support a contractual claim; the figure is not published for that period.
