Product

Monitoring and automated reporting. Nothing you didn't ask for.

Quorelia is a monitoring and automated daily/monthly reporting layer on top of your existing SCADA — not a maintenance dispatch or field-service tool. It watches your plants, calculates the figures your contracts and stakeholders need, and only publishes what it can defend.

Automated daily & monthly reports

Contractual availability, production, SOC, and downtime reports generated straight from plant data on your schedule — no manual pull, no manual formatting.

Defensible, provenance-backed figures

Every number carries its source channels, transformation, and assumptions. Each figure is re-derived by an independent path before publishing — if the two don't match, it doesn't go out.

Bilingual by default

Every report and client portal renders in English and Spanish out of the box — no separate translation workflow.

Coverage-aware verdicts

When data coverage falls below a defensible threshold, Quorelia withholds the contractual verdict instead of guessing — and says exactly why, on the first page, not in an appendix.

Client-ready exports

Branded PDF and dashboard views your clients can open directly — no reformatting on your side.

Works with your data sources

Integrates with common SCADA, inverter, and BESS/BMS monitoring platforms — solar or battery storage — so you keep your existing backend.

Alerts do the watching, so people don't have to

Quorelia raises automatic alerts the moment something needs attention — an inverter fault, a comms drop, a threshold breach — and routes them to the right owner. Your team gets dispatched to do the actual maintenance instead of staring at a screen 24/7/365.

Our philosophy

Why Quorelia exists

Ever watching. In service of the planet's energy.

Your energy. Always under watch.

We didn't set out to build a dashboard. We set out because someone was checking a screen at 2 a.m. so a number would be right at 8 a.m. Quorelia exists so that person can go build something instead — and the plant is still watched, still verified, still accounted for, every hour they're not there.

Vision

A world where no renewable energy asset operates unwatched, and where trusting a plant's numbers is the default — not something an investor has to independently verify.

Mission

Make every number a renewable asset reports provable — monitored continuously, verified independently, delivered on schedule — so the people responsible for it can trust it without re-checking it themselves.

Our approach

Automated AI monitoring absorbs the scope of work, with the plan and parameters tuned to each client's interest. Big-data reporting runs continuously — and every output is audited by senior professionals.

Our rule

Nothing gets published unless it can be explained to a person, in plain language, with its full trail of evidence attached. If the data isn't good enough to support a claim, we say so.
