# Quorelia — Project Files

Everything built so far for the Quorelia brand and product materials.

## Website
`quorelia-website.html` — bilingual (EN/ES) marketing site. Open directly in a browser. Uses `quorelia-banner.png` and `quorelia-hero-bg.mp4` from the same folder (keep all three together).

## SCADA_Dashboard
`bess-scada-dashboard.html` — interactive BESS monitoring dashboard sample (neutral/white-label, no Quorelia branding by design). Open directly in a browser.

## Presentations
- `Quorelia_Economic_Proposal.pptx/.pdf` — 12-slide pricing/value proposal for the Cavex plant stakeholders (internal, names the real plant figures).
- `Quorelia_Elevator_Pitch.pptx/.pdf` — 12-slide client-facing brand deck: who we are, mission/vision/objectives, four pillars, proof points, contact. Generic — safe to show any prospect.
- `Quorelia_Corporate_Identity_Manual.pptx/.pdf` — 22-slide brand standards manual: logo system, color, typography, business card, letterhead, email signature, templates.

## Documents
`AI_Usage_Rewards_Concept.docx/.pdf` — concept memo for an AI-token-usage rewards/points system.

## Brand_Assets
Logo files in PNG with transparent backgrounds:
- `quorelia-logo-full.png` — full color lockup (symbol + wordmark), **dark backgrounds only** (the wordmark is a light glow effect, illegible on white).
- `quorelia-symbol-color.png` — symbol only, full color, works on light or dark.
- `quorelia-logo-full-white.png` / `quorelia-logo-full-navy.png` — solid one-color lockups, for light or dark backgrounds respectively.
- `quorelia-symbol-white.png` / `quorelia-symbol-navy.png` — solid one-color symbol only.

## Open items / caveats
- **"Quorelia" is a proposed working name.** quorelia.com is already registered by a third party; materials assume the alternate domain **quorelia.io**, which has not been formally checked for availability or trademark conflicts — verify with a registrar and a real trademark search before public use.
- The Corporate Identity Manual's business card and email signature use a **placeholder name** ("Diego Fernández, Systems & Reporting Engineering") as an example — replace before printing or sending anything real.
- Figures in the Economic Proposal are internal/real; figures in the Elevator Pitch and CI Manual are deliberately genericized for external audiences.
