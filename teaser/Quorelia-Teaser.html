The problem

A modern energy asset produces
a continuous stream of signal.

SCADABESSInvertersAlarms AvailabilityState of chargeKPIsDeviations Energy productionCell behaviourMaintenance events Equipment statusHistorical trendsMeters WeatherCurtailmentWork ordersSpreadsheets DashboardsEmailMonthly reportsHuman interpretation

It arrives fragmented — across systems that were never designed to speak to each other, and formats that were never designed to be compared.

Data is abundant.
Intelligence is scarce.

More data does not automatically create better decisions. Operators and asset managers should not have to spend hours interpreting thousands of signals to understand what actually matters.
